About

Pharmacist by training. Agent builder by night.

I'm Frano, a pharmacist (MPharm) with nearly five years in pharmacometrics software. As a Senior Scientific Product Specialist at Simulations Plus in Paris, I build LLM agents for drug development and test them until scientists can trust what they produce. Before that I modeled drug release at Teva, and built Django sites as a freelancer on the side.
